PLATFORM OVERVIEW

Designed for Student Builders

A unified learning hub tailored for engineering students exploring AWS cloud architecture and software engineering.

What the LMS Is

The centralized learning environment hosted specifically for RGUKT Ongole student builders to access curated study materials, session recordings, and cloud exercise briefs.

What Students Can Do

Enroll in cohort workshops, track lab completion milestones, submit architectural project exercises, review quizzes, and verify their learning progress.

Learning Experience

Structured modules covering AWS core fundamentals, serverless computing, database paradigms, and cloud security tailored to complement university coursework.

Self-Paced Progression

Learn whenever you build. Access resources and reference architectures across academic terms at your own schedule with guided checkpoints.

Cohort Collaboration

Connect with fellow student builders, exchange debugging notes, and collaborate on hands-on team challenges under mentorship.

Why It's Useful

Bridges theoretical computer science concepts with industrial AWS cloud skills, preparing students for AWS certifications and technical engineering careers.
